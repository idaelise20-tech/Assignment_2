History

Rana FK is a football club based in Mo i Rana, Norway. The club was founded on December 4, 2017, following a merger between IL Stålkammeratene and Mo IL. The club has a strong focus on youth and junior development, with its youth and junior department established in 2018. The men’s top-level football initiative started in 2019 in the 4th division. Currently, the club has around 400 active members and holds accreditation as a quality club.

About the Club

Rana FK is a development-oriented club, actively striving for improvement across all aspects. Our passion lies in creating accessible activities with the highest possible quality, fostering a learning and enjoyable environment for children and youth.
